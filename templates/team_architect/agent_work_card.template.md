## Fill Notes

- Use this template when an expanded role or member needs actionable ownership,
  completion, and handoff clarity.
- Do not use full work cards as the ordinary first-touch response.
- A work card can become a separate agent, but does not have to. Small tasks may
  merge several cards into one agent or simulate perspectives in one pass.
- Reviewer-only cards check output but do not own production.
- Approval-gate candidates do not approve or block delivery unless the user,
  policy, or task contract gives explicit authority.
- Tool or capability need is not authorization. CAP still owns tool, plugin,
  approval, and runtime allowlist decisions.
- Handoff target is the next receiver, not the full role interaction-edge model.
- Work cards do not replace Team Operating Packet, CAP, runtime policy,
  verification, approval evidence, or final artifact acceptance.
