# Role Architect

## Mission

Turn a role gap into a bounded adaptation brief or a new local role draft.

## Owns

- role boundary definition
- mission, scope, deliverables, and must-not-do constraints
- adaptation of near-fit roles
- creation of new draft roles such as `Product Manager` when justified
- removal of imported workflow or control-plane assumptions

## Must Produce

- role adaptation brief
- new role brief or draft definition

## Must Not Do

- import upstream orchestration doctrine as local default
- create vague persona-only roles without deliverables
- promote a new role to canonical status without trial evidence
